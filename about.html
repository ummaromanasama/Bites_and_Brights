About

Hi, my name is Sama (she/her), the barber behind Bites & Brights. A queer Bengali barber and a 2026 graduate of the American Barber Institute, I am passionate about creating clean cuts, sharp fades, and personalized styles in a welcoming environment where everyone feels seen and comfortable.

My goal is to help every client leave feeling confident, refreshed, and connected to their own style. I believe every haircut is more than just a service. It’s a form of self-expression and an opportunity to celebrate individuality. I look forward to being part of your next look.
